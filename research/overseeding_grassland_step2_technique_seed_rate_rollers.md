# Overseeding grassland – Step 2: application technique, seed rate and rollers

*Technical dossier. Sources: WUR / Praktijkonderzoek, Louis Bolk Instituut, Teagasc, manufacturer documentation (Vredo, APV, Einböck, Hatzenbichler, Güttler, Amazone, Erth) and trade journals. Compiled 19 October 2026.*

> **Reader's guide:** Part A compares machine types. Part B follows the seed step by step from **the hopper through the metering unit, air and hoses to the ground**. Part C is about **rollers**. Part D contains the calibration test and a settings checklist.
> Where something comes from general agricultural engineering knowledge and not from a specific source, this is marked as *[general technique]*.

# PART A – Machine types for overseeding

| Type | How it works | Examples | Strong | Weak | Research results |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Double-disc slot seeder** | two discs set at an angle to each other cut a **V-slot**; the seed falls in, a press roller closes the slot | **Vredo** Agri / Agri Air / Agri Twin / SUPERcompact | seed in the soil with good contact; best seed distribution in the row (PR); relatively safe in drought too | slower (2.5 m ≈ 1 ha/h on older types); poor cutting in a dry top layer; open slits with wrong settings | PR: grass 239 (clay) / 349 (sand) seedlings/m²; PPS: 3–46 % emergence depending on conditions |
| **Strip-till** | milling elements mill strips **7 cm** wide at **23 cm** row spacing, in which seed is sown; ~¾ of the sward stays intact | Hunter Rotary Strip Seeder (2.75 m) | little competition in the strip; especially **best for clover** under poor conditions | heavy, slow, expensive; clover metering needs attention | PR: clover on clay 284/m² (best); Louis Bolk: 75 % clover cover after 3 months |
| **Harrow + pneumatic seeder (surface sowing)** | a harrow or grassland harrow opens the sward; seed travels through hoses to distribution plates and is spread widely; then rolled | Einböck Pneumaticstar, APV GP/GK + PS, Güttler GreenMaster, Hatzenbichler + Air, Saphir | wide (3–12 m+), fast, cheap per ha; one pass | seed lies on top and depends on rolling and moisture | Teagasc: 507 clover seedlings/m² (vs 591 direct drill) |
| **Harrow with seed tube behind the tine (tine seeder)** | seed is placed **directly behind each tine** in the freshly scratched soil | Güttler GreenMaster **Zinkensaat** (7.5 cm, patented) | better than surface sowing in tall crops and moist conditions (manufacturer) | new; little independent research | – |
| **Disc-coulter direct drill** | obliquely placed toothed discs cut a 10–15 mm slot; a trailing coulter (carbide) places the seed; a smooth roller controls depth | Erth Agriseeder (125 or 93 mm row spacing) | highest clover emergence in the Teagasc trial | most expensive method | Teagasc: **591** clover seedlings/m² |
| **Combination with a slurry injector** | seed mixed with manure or sent via pneumatics over the slurry slot | – | two passes in one | **bad for clover**, especially "mixed with manure" and on sand | PR: clover 41/m² (mixed) vs 157–284 with other machines |
| **Fertiliser spreader (broadcast)** | clover seed mixed with fertiliser (Teagasc: 0-7-30) and spread | any disc spreader | no investment | wind-sensitive, segregation in the hopper, no incorporation | Teagasc: 202 clover seedlings/m² (lowest) |

**Conclusions from the comparisons**
- PR 1997: with good execution, the differences between machines are **small for grass, large for clover**.
- SAC: "the machine you use makes little difference"; moisture and competition are decisive. This is at odds with Teagasc, where a machine with incorporation (drill or harrow + pneumatic) gave **2.5–3× as many clover seedlings** as broadcast spreading.
- WUR, main lesson: **settings and closing the slits** (rolling) weighed more heavily in 2019 than the machine type.

# PART B – The seed path: from hopper to ground

```
 ┌───────────────┐
 │ 1. HOPPER     │  agitator / auger against bridging and segregation
 └──────┬────────┘
        │  gravity
 ┌──────▼────────┐
 │ 2. METERING   │ fluted roller / toothed metering roller / cell wheel / flex roller
 │   (seed shaft)│  1 segment per outlet  ← drive: ground wheel or e-motor
 └──────┬────────┘
        │
 ┌──────▼────────┐        ┌──────────────┐
 │ 3. AIR DUCT   │◄───────│ FAN          │ electric 12 V / hydraulic / PTO
 │  (injector)   │        └──────────────┘
 └──────┬────────┘
        │  8 / 16 / 24 PVC hoses (optionally Y-splitters)
 ┌──────▼────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ 4. DELIVERY                                        │
 │  a) distribution plate (Prallteller) + splash guard → on top │
 │  b) seed tube behind harrow tine (Zinkensaat)      │
 │  c) seed pipe between disc pair → V-slot (Vredo)   │
 │  d) trailing coulter behind disc (Erth)            │
 └──────┬────────────────────────────────────────────┘
        │
 ┌──────▼────────┐
 │ 5. PRESSING   │ smooth roller / Cambridge / prism roller / press roller per row
 └───────────────┘
```

## B1. Hopper

| Brand/type | Capacity | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Vredo SUPERcompact | 235 l | **double auger** for even feed; calibration device per seed type |
| Vredo Agri Twin | ~200 l | pneumatic and mechanical, adjustable **3–210 kg/ha** |
| Vredo Agri Air (new) | **450 l** (was 220 l) | pneumatic hopper feeds directly to the **cam wheel**, which stays well filled even on slopes |
| Einböck P-BOX-MD/STI | 300 l steel (options 265 l plastic, 500 l) | **auger** against segregation and bridging; rubber seal (the hopper is under pressure); sight glass; drain hose directly above the seed wheel |
| APV PS 120/200/300 M1 | 120–300 l (up to 800+ l) | agitator standard |
| Hatzenbichler Air 8/16/24 | 200 (→400) / 400 (→600) / 800 l | **ball-bearing agitator** against bridging; level sensor |
| Amazone GreenDrill | 200 / 500 l | – |
| Güttler GreenMaster 800 | up to 640 l | – |

**Why the agitator matters:** grass mixtures are light and fluffy. They form bridges above the seed wheel, so the seed wheel runs "empty". Clover is small, round and heavy, and sinks to the bottom in a mixture (segregation). An auger keeps the feed constant and the mixture homogeneous. Teagasc advises, when spreading with fertiliser, to add the clover seed only in the field, because otherwise it sinks to the bottom of the spreader.

## B2. The metering unit (seed shaft / seed wheel / Säwelle)

This is where the **quantity** is determined. Almost all overseeding machines work **volumetrically**: per revolution of the shaft a fixed volume of seed is carried along. The rate in kg/ha therefore depends on the shaft speed relative to the forward speed, the working width and the **bulk density** of the seed. That is why a **calibration test** is needed for each seed batch (Part D).

### Types of metering units

| Type | Shape / operation | Where used | Suitable for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cam wheel / fluted roller** (*Nockenrad*) | cylinder with **axial grooves (flutes)**; the seed fills the grooves and falls out at the bottom. The rate is sometimes controlled via the **effective width** of the roller (a slide or spindle determines how many flutes are in the seed flow) and/or the speed | Vredo (Agri Air: "cam wheel"; SUPERcompact: spindle for adjustment); Kverneland **Accord** unit (used on older Erth Agriseeders) | grass and coarser seed; fine flutes for clover *[general technique]* |
| **Toothed metering roller, fine / coarse** (*Feinverzahnung / Grobverzahnung*) | roller with small (fine) or large (coarse) teeth or cells around the circumference, **a separate roller segment per hose**, so each outlet gets an equal partial flow | Einböck P-BOX (standard 1× fine + 1× coarse, **9 rollers in total** + special rollers; P-BOX-STI 600: rollers **B and E**, STI 1200: **D and F**), Hatzenbichler Air, APV PS (fine-seed shaft + coarse-seed shaft) | **fine:** clover, fine grasses, small amounts (from about **1 kg/ha**); **coarse:** grass mixtures, cereals, large amounts (up to ~300 kg/ha) |
| **Cell wheel / cell valve** (*Zellenrad*) | wheel with sealed cells of fixed volume. It **seals** the hopper at the same time against air pressure, so the fan air does not escape upwards into the hopper | Lehner Vento (cells of **2.5 ml** and **10 ml**, plus blank cells); Einböck Jumbo-Seed (2 metering units with cell wheel valve, each to 2 hoses); APV XD 4200: fine-seed shaft **192 cm³** and coarse-seed shaft **960 cm³** (presumably per revolution) | accurate at large volumes, also dosing under air pressure |
| **Flex roller** (*Flexwelle / flexible cells*) | cells with flexible (rubber/plastic) edges, so coarse or uneven seeds are not crushed or blocked | APV (Flex20 for coarse seed; 2 of the 8 optional shafts have flexible cells) | **mixtures with large and small seeds**, e.g. grass + clover or clover + cress |
| **Brush** | a brush above the metering roller strips off the excess or acts as a seal | air-seeder patents (urethane brush) | calibration accuracy, airtightness |

**Material:** stainless steel metering units are an option (Einböck, APV) and useful with fertiliser or coated seed (corrosion).

### Drive of the seed shaft

| System | How | Examples | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mechanical via ground wheel** | rubber ground wheel or track wheel → **flexible shaft** → seed roller. The rate is automatically proportional to the speed | Einböck P-BOX-MD (support wheel or track wheel; or an air-tyre wheel that turns on a roller), Hatzenbichler Air 8 | slip of the wheel on wet grass gives under-dosing; minimum bending radius of the flexible shaft (Einböck has a reversing piece) |
| **Electric, speed-controlled** | e-motor + **chain drive** (Einböck: chain tensioner, no belt) to the seed and auger shafts, controlled by a terminal | Einböck P-BOX-STI (SPEED-TRONIC), APV (Control Box 1.2/5.2/6.2/ISOBUS), Amazone GreenDrill (also task cards), Güttler SeedProfi | speed signal via **wheel sensor, radar, GPS or 7-pin tractor plug**; headland pre-dosing; shaft rotation sensor for control |

## B3. Air flow and fan

| Fan | Application | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Electric double, 12 V** | standard up to **6 m** working width (Einböck); APV PS 120 1–6 m, 8 outlets | Einböck, APV |
| **Electric "PLUS" / E-POWER** | wider or heavier seed, via ISOBUS plug or battery cable | Einböck, APV |
| **Hydraulic** | Einböck: max. **50 l/min**, pressure-free return, adjustable flow (Jumbo-Seed: 35 l/min); Vredo Agri Air (for rye, barley); APV 1–12 m, 16 outlets | Einböck, Vredo, APV |
| **PTO** | standard above 6 m (Einböck P-BOX-STI 1200) | Einböck, APV |

**Function:** the air serves only to **transport** the metered seed through the hoses. The dosing happens before the air.

- **Too little air:** seed stays in sagging hoses and blocks, especially heavy seed (Einböck: with 6 m and heavy seed sometimes only 3 m possible without a stronger fan).
- **Too much air:** the seed bounces off the distribution plate and flies away. That is why there is a splash guard.
- Option: **airflow monitoring** and a fan speed sensor (Einböck, APV).

**At Vredo:** the older Agri concept works without air. The seed falls by gravity from the metering wheel through hoses or pipes to the disc pairs. **Agri Air** adds air support. In the PPS trial, an Agri Air was used (2016–18) and a machine without air (2019). The best result (2019) came *without* air support, but with extra rolling. The machine type was therefore not the limiting factor.

## B4. Hoses and distribution

| Feature | Values | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Number of outlets | **8** (standard up to 6 m), **16** (12 m), **24** (Hatzenbichler Air 24, up to 18 m) | Einböck, APV, Hatzenbichler |
| Splitting | above 6 m hoses are split (**Y-pieces / doubling pieces**) to 12 or 16 outlets | Einböck, APV |
| Vredo distributor | distributors switchable from **10 to 5 outlets** (when elements are lifted) | Vredo / FarmingUK |
| Material | **PVC or plastic seed hose** | Einböck, APV |
| Supplied length | 24 m (Einböck 6 m) / 40 m (Einböck 12 m) / 25 m roll (APV) / 50 m (Hatzenbichler Air 16) | manufacturers |
| Diameter | APV XD 4200 (large volume): distribution plates with **45–50 mm** hose inlet. For grass and clover machines the manufacturers give no diameter. Common is about 25–35 mm *[general technique, not confirmed]* | APV |

**Hose installation rules** *[general technique, recommended by manufacturers in installation manuals]*:
1. **As short and as equal in length as possible**: unequal lengths give different resistance and therefore an uneven distribution across the working width.
2. **Lay continuously falling**, **no sagging loops**: seed collects in them ("clogging").
3. **Wide bends**, no kinks.
4. At a **Y-split** branch off symmetrically, so both branches are the same length.
5. Secure the hoses against vibration and rubbing (wear of PVC by quartz grains in coated seed).

## B5. Delivery onto or into the ground

### a) Distribution plate / Prallteller (surface sowing)
- The seed shoots out of the hose onto an **inclined plate** and fans out over a strip. At Einböck the plate is placed **centrally** between the tines.
- Einböck: the plate holder is **infinitely adjustable in angle**. There is a **height-adjustable splash guard** with a **slot**, so it can be set for each seed type and the seed does not bounce up.
- APV: 8 distribution plates on 4 hexagonal bars of 1.2 m.
- Advantage over a fertiliser spreader: largely **wind-insensitive**, because the seed is released close to the ground.
- Setting *[general technique]*: the plate as low as possible and the angle such that the fans of neighbouring plates just overlap. Fine seed (clover) bounces further than light grass seed, so check at every change.

### b) Seed tube behind the harrow tine (Güttler Zinkensaat)
- The seed is placed **directly behind each tine**, close to the ground, in the freshly scratched strip.
- Tine spacing **7.5 cm**. The GreenMaster 300 has 12 mm spring tines at 75 mm, 2 rows.

### c) Seed pipe between disc pair (Vredo)
- Two discs (Ø approx. **25 cm** on the SUPERcompact) are set in a V-position with a **progressive compression spring** per element. They cut a V-slot. The seed falls through the seed pipe between the discs into the slot, and the soil clamps it in.
- **Row spacing 7.5 cm** (Agri) or 10/7.5/5/3.5 cm (sports pitch). Sowing depth adjustable, about 0.5–2 cm (PR: Vredo 0.5–2 cm). Sports pitch versions have a **depth skid** and a leading roller for depth.
- **Cutting in** is essential. In 2017 the machine cut in well, but the seed was distributed less evenly in the slit (wet soil).

### d) Coulter behind disc (Erth Agriseeder)
- Toothed discs on two rows, at an angle, cut a **10–15 mm** slot and loosen some soil.
- A **trailing coulter with a carbide tip**, pressed against the disc with rubber rings, places the seed.

### e) Strip-till (Hunter)
- Milling element **7 cm** wide, row spacing **23 cm**, sowing depth about 4 cm (PR).

# PART C – Rollers and pressing

Rolling is one of the most important success factors in all sources.

- **Teagasc:** "soil contact … one of the most crucial factors".
- **WUR 2019:** extra rolling after overseeding, with the press roller set deeper, closed the slits completely. This brought emergence to **46 %**, against 3–10 % in the years without extra rolling. The researchers attribute the better result mainly to this extra rolling.

## C1. Roller types

| Roller | Shape | Dimensions (sources) | Operation | Use in overseeding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Smooth press roller** (on the machine) | smooth steel or plastic cylinder, often with a **self-adjusting scraper** | Vredo SUPERcompact: **Ø 22 cm**; Vredo Agri Twin 4.4 m: smooth roller; Erth: smooth roller also serves as **depth guide** | presses the slot shut and levels | standard on disc seeders. Used in the PPS trial in 2016 and 2019. In 2019 set deeper and rolled a **second time** |
| **Cambridge roller** (ring roller) | loose **rings with a V-profile** that turn freely on the axle, often alternating smooth and toothed | depends on brand; rings commonly Ø 45–56 cm *[general technique]* | presses in ridges and crumbles; the freely turning rings follow the ground and clean themselves | PPS 2017–2018 as press roller on the Vredo; WUR and Barenbrug advise **rolling afterwards with a Cambridge roller**. The ridge pattern retains moisture and limits erosion |
| **Prism roller** (Güttler) | rings with **prism-shaped points**, self-cleaning | Güttler GreenMaster: **33/38 cm** and **45/50 cm** (inner/outer Ø); Mayor/Master: **45/50 cm**; Matador: 50/45 cm; Mediana 30 on the GreenMaster 300 | pushes back frost-lifted or loosened sward, presses the seed in, **promotes tillering** | behind the harrow + pneumatic; according to Güttler (Aulendorf trial station) a post-sowing success "comparable to slot sowing" (manufacturer claim) |
| **Heavy flat grassland roller** | smooth, filled roller | – | levelling in spring | less suitable directly after sowing on wet soil (compaction, smooth crust) *[general technique]* |
| **Livestock (trampling)** | – | – | Teagasc: "roll or trample the seed in"; sheep as a roller | with broadcast sowing on grazed land |

## C2. Choice of roller by situation (synthesis)

| Situation | Advice |
|---|---|
| Disc seeder (Vredo), dry or normal top layer | press roller set well; in drought an **extra rolling pass** (WUR 2019) or a Cambridge roller |
| Disc seeder, slits stay open | set press roller lower, check disc pressure, roll afterwards |
| Harrow + pneumatic (surface sown) | **always** a prism roller or Cambridge roller in the same pass |
| Broadcast clover | roll (Teagasc: 4 days after sowing, then light water or manure) or let it be trampled |
| Very wet soil | do not roll or sow; postpone (slits smear shut) |

# PART D – Calibration (catch test) and settings checklist

## D1. Catch test – steps (APV / Einböck, common procedure)

1. Choose the right **seed roller**: fine for clover and low rates, coarse or flex for grass mixtures.
2. Enter **working width, intended forward speed and kg/ha** in the terminal (APV asks this every time).
3. Place a catch tray or bag under the metering unit (catch slide or plate open).
4. Run with the **catch button** on the machine or in the terminal until a clear amount is in the bag.
5. **Weigh** with the supplied digital scale and enter the weight. The computer calculates the factor (grams per revolution).
6. If the deviation is large, do a **second test** (APV).
7. **Repeat for each seed batch**: tetraploid and diploid differ in bulk density, coated seed too, and clover is much heavier than grass.
8. Field check: after a known area in hectares, weigh the remainder in the hopper.

For mechanical machines (ground wheel) *[general technique]*: turn the wheel a calculated number of revolutions corresponding to a known area (e.g. 1/40 ha), and catch and weigh the seed.

## D2. Field settings checklist (WUR Factsheet 9, extended)

- [ ] **Test strip** sown (WUR) and checked:
  - [ ] **cutting in** (do the discs or tines cut through the felt layer?)
  - [ ] **sowing depth**: grass 12–15 mm, clover ~10 mm, never deeper than 3–4 cm
  - [ ] **seed distribution in the slit** (Vredo) or across the working width (distribution plates)
  - [ ] **closing of the slits** (press roller)
- [ ] All hoses clear? Equal seed per outlet? (briefly catch with a bag per hose)
- [ ] Fan speed suitable for the seed type (no seed bouncing away, no clogging)
- [ ] Splash guard and plate angle set for the seed type
- [ ] Forward speed constant (ground wheel: slip? electric: speed signal correct?)
- [ ] Harrow intensity: Einböck has a central lever with **5 positions**; Güttler offers an optional ripper board. On stony soil straight tines; on rough sward sprung levelling plates at the front and 10 mm tines (Einböck)
- [ ] After sowing: roll, optionally a second rolling pass
